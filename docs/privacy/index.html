In short: no account, no ads, no tracking. Profiles and learning progress are stored only on your phone. The only data that leaves the phone is crash reports without personal information and the purchase information needed to verify a subscription. We never sell data.

Who we are

LINGOYUN is a language-learning app made by {{DEVELOPER_NAME}}. For questions about this policy or to use your rights: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

Data that stays on your phone

  • Profiles: nickname, age group (2–7, 7–12, adult), language studied and level. We never ask for a real name, birth date, photo or contact details.
  • Learning progress: answered words, review schedule, XP, gems, streak and badges.
  • Settings: sound and vibration preferences.

This data is kept only on your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting a profile in Settings deletes its progress; deleting the app deletes everything.

Data that leaves your phone

Crash reports (Sentry). If the app runs into an error, the device model, operating system, app version and technical details of the error are sent to Sentry. Reports never include nicknames, profile data, screenshots or a user identifier, and IP addresses are not stored. Reports are kept for at most 90 days and used only to fix bugs.

Subscription (Apple, Google and RevenueCat). The App Store or Google Play takes the payment; your card details never reach us. To check whether your subscription is active, RevenueCat receives the purchase record and an anonymous identifier created by the app. On iOS the free 7-day trial is recorded as a free purchase on your Apple account.

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools. Your data is not used for advertising or profiling, and is not sold or shared.

Children

LINGOYUN is designed for families; parents create children's profiles. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children (US COPPA, EU GDPR Article 8). Nicknames stay on the device. Purchases and settings sit behind a parental gate that an adult can solve. The app has no chat, sharing, external links or ads.

Your rights (GDPR and Turkish KVKK)

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to object to processing and to complain to a data protection authority. You can delete the data on your phone yourself, in the app or by deleting the app. For crash reports and subscription records, write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}; we answer within 30 days.

Legal basis: legitimate interest in keeping the app working for crash reports; performance of a contract for subscription checks.

Changes

If we change this policy, we publish the new version on this page with its effective date and announce important changes in the app.
